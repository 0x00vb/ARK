# Plugin artwork

Gmail and Google Calendar marks are Google product artwork. Bundled unmodified from https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/2x/gmail_48dp.png and https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/2x/calendar_48dp.png, used only to identify their integrations.

module-0.svg, module-1.svg and module-2.svg are original ARK artwork for plugins without a bundled brand mark.
