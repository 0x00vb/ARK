# Review writing rules
- [W1] (always) Quote figures exactly as the source states them, with their unit.
- [W2] (always) Say which document a claim comes from.
- [W3] Separate what changed from what stayed the same. #compare #comparison #change
- [W4] Name an owner or a next step for every problem you list. #problems #actions
- [W5] Keep a summary under 150 words. #summary #summarize
