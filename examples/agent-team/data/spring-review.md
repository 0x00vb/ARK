# Spring review: support team

The team answered 4,120 requests between March and May, up from 3,380 in winter.
Median first reply time was 3 hours 10 minutes; the goal was 2 hours.

What went well:
- The new help articles cut repeat questions about billing by about a third.
- Weekend coverage by two volunteers kept the Monday backlog under 90 requests.

What did not:
- Two people left in April and their replacements started only in late May.
- Requests about account access doubled after the login change on 14 April.

Next quarter: hire one more person, write five articles on account access, and
try a shared on-call rotation for weekends.
