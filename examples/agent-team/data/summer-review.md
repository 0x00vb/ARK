# Summer review: support team

The team answered 4,610 requests between June and August.
Median first reply time fell to 1 hour 55 minutes, inside the 2 hour goal for the first time.

What went well:
- Five account access articles shipped in June; access requests fell from 610 to 240 a month.
- The weekend rotation replaced the volunteers and nobody worked more than two weekends.

What did not:
- The hiring plan slipped: the new position is still open.
- A pricing change in July brought 380 billing questions in one week.

Next quarter: fill the open position, prepare articles before any pricing change,
and measure customer satisfaction, which nobody tracks today.
